1. Core Game Systems
Turns

Players receive 4 turns every 5 minutes, capped at 5,000 turns.
Nearly every action (training, recruiting, attacking, buying/selling, traveling, etc.) costs turns.
Implementation: Use a background job (cron every minute) that adds turns based on last_turn_update timestamp. Cap at 5000. Store turns and last_turn_update per character.

Multi-Character Accounts

One account can control multiple characters (common for specialized roles: pure Thug, Pimp, Dealer, hybrids like Thimp/Thealer).
Enforce household/multi-account rules in ToS + basic IP/device checks for anti-abuse.

Countries / Locations

Multiple countries with separate rankings and competition. Travel costs turns. Dominance bonuses for gangs (e.g., Dominant, Presence, Emerging status).
Track player location; attacks easier/safer in same country.

Total Value & Rankings

Total Value = Cash + Bank + Drug Value (current market price × quantity) + Follower Value.
Separate rankings: Overall, Thugs, Pimps, Dealers, Gangs (Global + per country).
Top 100 per category. "Targets in Range" based on value proximity (Top 15/20 Rule for big hits).

2. Character Progression (Character Page)
Attributes & Skills (train with turns + cash)

Base: Strength (Thugs), Intelligence (Dealers), Charisma (Pimps).
Life Skills:
Guerrilla Warfare (Thug combat).
Chemistry (Dealer production/trading).
Women's Studies (Pimp).
Sixth Sense (defense, detection, dealer bonuses).
Sexual Education (Pimp).


Warfare Ratings (main power metric):

Thug Warfare ≈ Strength + Guerrilla Warfare + upgrades.
Dealer Warfare ≈ Intelligence + Chemistry + Sixth Sense + upgrades.
Pimp Warfare ≈ Charisma + Sexual Education + Women's Studies + upgrades.

Upgrades (one-time cash purchases, permanent bonuses):

Thug: M-16 (attack), Humvee (raid/loot).
Dealer: Private Jet, Meth Lab.
Pimp: Private Doctor, Penthouse.
Max or balance upgrades per role.

Training: Costs turns + cash. Focus on relevant stats for role (e.g., pure Thugs dump almost everything into Guerrilla Warfare after minimal Strength).
3. Economy
Drug Market (shared, global)

Fluctuating prices per drug (random walk + mean reversion + news events).
Features: Current price, hourly % change, 48h history chart per drug, News Stories (major influencers).
Buy/Sell actions: Buy Max, Buy X, Sell, Sell All (type), Sell All.
Drug Dealers produce drugs passively (rate based on stats + upgrades).
Bank: Safe storage; withdraw costs turns.

Other Income

Pimps + Hoes → steady cash.
Gang Holdings (Casinos = big passive income; set payout to gang or self).
Raids/looting.

4. Followers & Recruiting (War Room)
Recruit with turns (success rate based on stats, current followers, role).

Thugs (OGs): Combat power.
Drug Dealers: Passive drug production.
Hoes (Escorts/Webcam/etc. for Pimps): Income + special attacks.
Mercenaries: Temporary hired power (expensive, consumes drugs, good for defense spikes).

5. Combat & Attacks (War Room)
Limits: Attacks Out (max ~15, regenerates), Attacks In (daily reset).
Key Attacks (success based on Warfare Rating difference + followers + RNG):

Spy (safe, low cost): Reveals target stats, followers, holdings.
Thug: House Raid (steal cash/drugs), Intimidate (force flee/waste turns).
Dealer: Steal Dealers, Tainted Drugs (weaken enemy), Drug Love (debuff enemy Guerrilla Warfare).
Pimp: Steal Hoes, Diseased Hoes (weaken/kill enemy followers), Turf War (isolate target).

12-Hour Rule (retaliation window): Attacked players get up to 12 free attacks back within 12 hours (attacks decay over time). Spying usually doesn't trigger it.
Top 15/20 Rule: Easier to hit players near you in value/rankings. Used for coordinated gang drains.
Defense/Status Effects: Intimidate, Turf War, Drug Love create debuffs that cost turns to break (train relevant stats to resist).
6. Gangs (Social & Power Multiplier)

Create/join (requests, invites).
Roles: Godfather (full control), Underboss.
Holdings: Casinos (income), Barracks (recruiting/defense), Airliners (travel bonuses?).
Shared gang bank, donations, casino payouts.
Alliances between gangs.
Country dominance bonuses.

Strategy Meta: Most success comes from 20–25+ character gangs coordinating attacks, 12-houring, market plays, and holdings.
7. Other Pages & Features

Messages: Personal, Attack In/Out logs, Gang logs.
Rankings & Top 100.
Chat / FU Line (community).
Account management: Donations (bonus turns), password, etc.

8. Technical Implementation (Backend Focus)
Stack Recommendation

Backend: Node.js (NestJS) or Python (FastAPI).
DB: PostgreSQL (strong transactions) + Redis (caching prices, sessions, queues).
Jobs: BullMQ / Celery for turns, price updates, production.
Frontend: Modern React/Vue + Tailwind (text-heavy but clean UI; add mobile support).

Key Backend Components

Turn System — Timestamp-based regeneration.
Drug Market Engine — Background job every 5–15 min: random walk + news events + history logging. Redis cache for live prices.
Combat Engine — Atomic transactions: calculate success % from Warfare diff + followers + upgrades + RNG. Apply loot, debuffs, logs.
Follower Production — Tick-based or on-demand.
Rankings — Cached recalc on value changes.
Anti-Cheat — Action rate limits, turn timestamps, multi-account detection.

Formulas to Approximate (reverse-engineer via playtesting):

Success rate ≈ (Attacker Warfare / Defender Warfare) × modifiers (followers, upgrades, debuffs).
Loot % based on raid success and defender thugs left.
Production rates scale with stats + upgrades.
