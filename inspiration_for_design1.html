Automating
weekly LVB shipments

Every week MOJOGEAR spends 1–2 hours manually deciding which products to ship to Bol.com's warehouse. The right data, a smart algorithm, and a human checkpoint can turn this into a 10-minute review.

The problem

What's happening now, every single week

1

Manual data gathering

Someone opens 4–5 different systems — Bol.com, ProductFlow, Exact Online, Picqer, Airtable — and copies data into spreadsheets by hand.

2

Guesswork on quantities

Decisions are made based on gut feeling and rough mental math. Too much stock sent to Bol means own warehouse runs dry. Too little means Bol sells out.

3

1–2 hours per week

This repetitive task eats time every week. It's error-prone, hard to scale, and depends on one person's availability and mental state.

The solution

Automated data pipeline + smart logic + human approval

Step 2 — Smart Quantity Calculation

A

Calculate days of stock at Bol

Bol stock ÷ avg. daily Bol sales = days until Bol sells out

B

Determine target shipment

Target = (days until next shipment + safety buffer) × avg. daily Bol sales − current Bol stock

C

Cap by available own stock

Never send more than what's in Picqer, minus B2B backlog from Exact Online. Reserve stock for own channels.

D

Round to lot sizes

Round up to nearest full box (from Airtable lot size). If the product barely sells on Bol, skip it entirely.

Step 3 — Human Review & Action

Approve & Ship

Everything looks good? One click exports the shipment list — SKUs, EANs, quantities — all formatted and ready for dispatch.

Flag & Follow Up

Something's off — supplier delay, low own stock, weird sales spike? Flag it, describe what's needed, assign someone, and it auto-appears next week.

The Follow-Up Loop

When things can't be fixed this week — here's how the team stays aligned

1. Flag & Describe

Click the yellow button on any product. Describe what's wrong and what needs to happen. Assign a responsible person.

2. Notify & Track

n8n sends a Slack/Teams message to the assigned person with context: what product, what's needed, and by when (next shipment).

3. Track in History

Every approved shipment is saved with a full manifest. Use it to verify what was sent, spot patterns, or look back at past decisions.

Technical approach

Tools, APIs, and integration strategy

Orchestration

n8n — central workflow engine

All API credentials already available. Runs the weekly data fetch, calculation, and notification workflows. Can be triggered on schedule or manually.

APIs Used

Picqer API — real-time stock levels
ProductFlow API — sales history & velocity
Bol.com API — Bol stock, orders, LVB shipments
Airtable API — lot sizes, product metadata
Exact Online API — B2B order backlog

Interface

n8n Form or Web App — human review

A simple form-based interface where the team reviews calculated quantities, edits if needed, and approves or flags. Could also be built as a lightweight web app or Airtable interface.

Notifications

Slack / Teams — flag alerts

When a product is flagged, the assigned person gets a message with product details, the issue, and what action is needed. Open flags are summarized in the weekly shipment notification.
