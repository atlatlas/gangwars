Game Features

Everything you need to build your empire and dominate the city.

Crime System
Pull off heists, street deals, and assassinations. Each crime tests a different stat with dynamic success chances.
PvP Combat
Ambush, mug, or hit rivals. Spy for intel, steal their cash, and climb the respect leaderboard.
Skill Training
Master Guerrilla Warfare, Chemistry, Sixth Sense, and more. Every skill unlocks new income multipliers.
Drug Market
Buy low, sell high in a live player-driven economy. Prices fluctuate with supply, demand, and in-game events.
Gangs
Form a crew, invite allies, climb the ranks from Member to Leader. Dominate the streets together.
Street Economy
Recruit and manage workers, collect passive income, and build your empire one block at a time.
Banking
Deposit cash to keep it safe from attackers. Earn passive interest and grow your net worth.
Casino
Test your luck at Blackjack, Slots, and Ride the Bus. Walk out rich or walk out broke.
